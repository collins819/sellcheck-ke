How it works

Four steps, before any money changes hands

SellCheck KE is an information collection and pre-screening tool — not a verification authority. It structures what a seller discloses so a buyer can review it before arranging a physical inspection.

01

Seller provides item information

Details, condition, history and photo links, organised by category.

02

SellCheck generates a reference

A unique reference number such as SC-KE-2026-00001 is issued instantly.

03

Buyer reviews the submission

The buyer looks up the reference and reads what the seller disclosed.

04

Buyer arranges verification

Physical inspection, ownership checks and professional verification follow — before purchase.

What SellCheck is not

An information stub, not a guarantee

!

Important notice. SellCheck KE collects information provided by sellers to help buyers conduct a preliminary review before arranging an inspection or purchase. Submission of information does not confirm ownership, authenticity, roadworthiness, legal status, condition, or value of an item. Buyers should independently verify documents and inspect items before making payment. For vehicles and motorcycles, arrange independent mechanical and ownership verification. For property, conduct an official land registry search and use qualified legal assistance.

Privacy notice

SellCheck KE collects only the information needed to generate a pre-screening submission: seller contact details, item information, and photo/document links you choose to provide. We do not collect passwords, PINs, bank details, or national ID uploads. Contact details are shown to administrators and, where appropriate, to a genuine buyer arranging an inspection — not published openly. Data is stored in a Google Sheet accessible only to the platform administrator. You may request removal of your submission by contacting the administrator with your reference number.

Terms & disclaimer

SellCheck KE is an information collection and pre-screening platform. It is not a government agency, licensing body, escrow service, or verification authority. Submission of information by a seller does not confirm ownership, authenticity, roadworthiness, legal status, condition, or value of any item. The "Information completeness" score reflects only how much requested information was provided — never authenticity or safety. Buyers are responsible for independently verifying documents, inspecting items in person, and, for vehicles, motorcycles and property, obtaining professional or legal verification before making any payment. SellCheck KE is not liable for losses arising from transactions between buyers and sellers.
